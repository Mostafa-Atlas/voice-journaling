# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We pledge to make participation in this project a harassment-free experience
for everyone, regardless of age, body size, disability, ethnicity, gender
identity and expression, level of experience, nationality, personal appearance,
race, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

## Our standards

- Be respectful, constructive, and patient.
- No harassment, insults, sexualized language, or unwanted attention.
- No publishing of private information (including voice data, transcripts,
  user IDs, or credentials) without explicit consent.
- Assume good faith; disagree about ideas, not people.

## Scope

This applies in issues, pull requests, discussions, and any project space.

## Enforcement

Violations can be reported privately to the repository owner. All reports are
handled confidentially. Maintainers may remove content and, for serious or
repeated violations, temporarily or permanently ban contributors.

Do not include credentials, recordings, or transcripts in a report.
See `SECURITY.md` for how to report vulnerabilities privately.
